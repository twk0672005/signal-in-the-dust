## User Scenarios & Testing
### User Story 1 - Enter once (Priority: P1)
A prospective client sees full-screen Aeral concept art, chooses New/Continue/settings, watches a planet-scanning ring with truthful progress, and enters the chosen experience without a second Start menu.
**Independent Test**: Cold/warm load, valid/invalid Continue, New confirmation, download failure/retry, both languages and reduced motion.
**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. Home art/actions render before heavy gameplay assets are required.
2. Selected launch intent is consumed once after readiness; Continue failure never silently clears progress.
3. Unknown progress and initialization are labeled honestly rather than filled with a fake percentage.

### User Story 2 - Freely explore a coherent ecology (Priority: P1)
Drive four distinct blue-violet luminous habitats; observe large original fauna, small ground/flying organisms and six flora families with grounded roots, layered material detail, water/shore transitions and near/mid/far depth.
**Independent Test**: Continuous real-input route, both cameras, turns, shore approach and return view; matched A/B/C baseline and low-fog diagnostic.
**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. No compulsory four-survey/eight-note chain is needed to tour content.
2. Near/side/rear/driving views retain readable anatomy, motion and materials against approved concepts.
3. Existing driving, corrected steering/brake, V, touch, languages, ecological responses and saves remain usable.
4. Veyra/Aeral/Morrow are substantially redesigned; Lithara is a fourth large low asymmetrical sedimentary landform with four arched support limbs and dormant/awake poses, distinct from six-limbed Veyra.

### User Story 3 - Understand without a task wall (Priority: P1)
Use a restrained heading-correct top-right map, one contextual short whisper and subtle sound, essential interaction feedback and pause/settings/contact author.
**Independent Test**: Region/alarm/recovery transitions, whisper dedupe/cooldown/disable, map heading, mute/reduced motion, pause contact.
**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. One relevant localized message appears without repetition; authored guidance never claims live generative intelligence.
2. Map conveys direction without teleport.
3. Pause contact opens a composer for TWK0672005@gmail.com and never sends automatically.

### User Story 4 - Usable quality tiers (Priority: P1)
Desktop prioritizes fidelity; mobile retains usable low quality and an honest explanation of desktop detail.
**Independent Test**: Exported 1080p standard, 720p low, landscape touch emulation; label physical-device gaps.
**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. Menus/loading/gameplay remain readable; errors and retry work.
2. Low preserves interactive objects, recognizable species, navigability and saved preferences.

### Edge Cases
Repeated startup; corrupt/legacy/blocked storage; blur/pause/portrait; animation interruption; transparent overlap; backlighting; dense canopy; saved completed encounters; source/probe/runtime evidence mismatch.
