# Product contract — 2026-09-19
Goal: approximately 30 minutes of meaningful player exploration in four connected Godot regions, with English/Traditional Chinese, throttle driving, speedometer and V first/third-person cameras.
Regions: Aurora Shelf (frost/aurora), Ember Rift (geothermal crystals), Veil Marsh (fog/luminous wetland), Pale Decay (spore/decaying geology).
The four regions remain freely reachable. Travel, observation, creature responses, optional side routes and Root Choir resolution must supply the playtime; no artificial countdowns or enforced slow driving.
Current 800m road at8m/s is about100 seconds of ideal straight travel: it is a development skeleton, NOT 30-minute proof.
Biology: distinct Veyra Lithovore, Aeral Veil, Morrow Shell; Root Choir is the mineral communication network. Speed/light/observation must change visible behavior, not only HUD labels.
Controls: W throttle, S opposite-direction braking/reverse, Space service brake, A/D steering, V camera, E observe/transmit, Esc pause, R reset confirmation.
Runtime: Godot4.7.2, GDScript, Compatibility, single-thread Web. Preserve useful current code/assets; frozen legacy is not edited.
Asset reuse: project-authored GLBs/samples; OFL Noto subset; verified CC0 demo textures are eligible. Terrain3D is isolated authoring research pending actual extension-load/Web tests.
Non-goals: combat, inventories, economy, multiplayer, live LLM dependencies.
Evidence: native rule tests plus actual uninterrupted player routes, first/third-person and four distinct region captures, audio, performance on declared hardware, independent human review and private-site readback.
Performance targets remain60FPS/1080p, p95<=25ms standard and>=30FPS/720p low; capture/test overhead must be separated in a dedicated profile, not silently filtered from samples.
Public release only after required player/platform/artifact gates. Missing actual human/macOS/browser evidence stays PARTIAL.
