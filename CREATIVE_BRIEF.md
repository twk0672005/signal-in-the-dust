# Creative brief
Grounded, restrained alien biosphere at the edge of a storm. Four habitat signatures: auroral frost,
thermal crystal rift, charged fog marsh and pale spore decay. Charcoal basalt, oxidized warm dust,
indigo-black sky. Off-white worn six-wheel rover and amber instruments. Cyan belongs to the signal;
organisms use their own rust, amber, violet and mineral tones.
Authored near/mid/far geology with deliberate path and strong silhouette of an organic mineral antenna.
Arrival exterior establishes rover, then first-person sensor mast view with tactile instrument hood.
Signal answers a player pulse: unfolding ribs, subsurface roots and distant environmental echoes.
Sound uses local sampled wind, wheels, servos and resonant pulses. Speed changes wheel/servo layers;
each habitat adds one restrained ecological rhythm. Reduced motion removes camera bob and avoids shake.
